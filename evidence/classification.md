# Evidence Classification Stage

Categorizes evidence into 4 distinct levels to prevent strategic hallucinations.

## Classification Levels

### Level 1 — Verified Fact
Directly supported by a reliable, official primary source.
- *Example*: Competitor X launched Product Y on August 12.
- *Source*: Official press release.

### Level 2 — Observed Signal
Measurable, observable, but not explicitly stated.
- *Example*: Competitor X increased Pro plan price from $49 to $59.
- *Evidence*: Pricing page snapshot.

### Level 3 — Inference
A reasonable interpretation derived from multiple signals.
- *Example*: The pricing change may indicate a shift toward enterprise customers.

### Level 4 — Hypothesis
A strategic possibility requiring further validation.
- *Example*: Competitor X may be preparing to enter the enterprise security market.

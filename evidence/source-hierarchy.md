# Source Hierarchy

Defines trustworthiness based on source type.

## Tiers

### Tier 1: Primary Sources (Highest Trust)
- Official company websites.
- Official product announcements & press releases.
- Technical documentation & APIs.
- Official pricing pages.
- Regulatory filings (SEC, etc.).

### Tier 2: High-Quality Independent Sources
- Reputable journalism.
- Industry publications.
- Analyst research (Gartner, Forrester).

### Tier 3: Community Sources
- Reddit, developer forums, public Discord servers.
- App store reviews & G2/Capterra ratings.

### Tier 4: Low-Confidence Sources
- Anonymous posts.
- Scraped aggregators.
- Unattributed claims.

# Confidence Model

Establishes how confidence levels are calculated and assigned.

## Levels of Confidence

- **HIGH**: Multiple primary sources or direct primary source corroboration.
- **MEDIUM**: Reliable secondary evidence or multiple weaker signals.
- **LOW**: Single weak source or ambiguous evidence.
- **UNVERIFIED**: Claims that require further validation before being relied upon.

## Confidence Allocation Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Confidence applies to the **claim**, not to the source. A high-tier source can publish a low-confidence speculative claim.

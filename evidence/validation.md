# Evidence Validation Stage

Ensures that collected content is authentic and fit for classification.

## Validation Gates
1. **Source Corroboration**: Verify that the domain matches known official endpoints or reliable sources.
2. **Freshness Guard**: Check date of publication vs. current system date. Discard outdated information unless conducting historical temporal analysis.
3. **Data Integrity Check**: Verify metadata completeness (e.g., must contain a source identifier, description, and observed date).

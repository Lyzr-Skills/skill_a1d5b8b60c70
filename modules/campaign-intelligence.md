# Campaign Intelligence Module

Analyzes marketing push strategies, advertising efforts, and narrative distribution.

## Key Channels
- Search and display ad patterns (Google Ads, LinkedIn).
- Social media push topics (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, YouTube).
- Content strategy (whitepapers, eBooks, search keywords targeted).
- Event sponsorship and speaking gigs.

## Key Question
What core marketing narrative is this competitor attempting to establish in this cycle?

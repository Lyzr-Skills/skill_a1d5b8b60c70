# Positioning Intelligence Module

Analyzes how competitors message their value and identity to the market.

## Key Focus Areas
- **Primary Positioning**: Foundational identity claim (e.g., "AI infrastructure for enterprise").
- **Target Persona**: Who they address directly.
- **Category Claims**: Defining their product category.
- **Differentiators**: Declared unique advantages.
- **Messaging Themes**: Core topics emphasized on marketing channels.

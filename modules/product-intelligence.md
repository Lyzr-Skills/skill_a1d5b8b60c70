# Product Intelligence Module

Monitors technological additions, feature enhancements, and roadmap changes.

## Tracking Metrics
- New capabilities or APIs.
- Deprecated or removed features.
- Beta programs and early access features.
- Third-party integrations and ecosystem partnerships.

## Change Extraction Flow
```
Previous Product State
      ↓
Current Product State
      ↓
Extracted Product Changes
      ↓
Hypothesized Strategic Intent
```

# Opportunity Detection Module

Discovers strategic market gaps and areas where competitors show weakness.

## Opportunity Categories
- **Market Gaps**: Customer requests unaddressed by any player.
- **Competitor Weaknesses**: Consistent complaints/outages in competitor platforms.
- **Pricing Gaps**: Underserved price points.
- **Product Gaps**: Missing functionality.
- **Positioning Gaps**: Weak or unowned brand claims.
- **Distribution Gaps**: Unexploited marketing and sales channels.

## Opportunity Scoring
Prioritize market opportunities objectively:
$$\text{Opportunity Score} = \text{Market Potential} \times \text{Strategic Fit} \times \text{Feasibility} \times \text{Confidence}$$

- **Market Potential**: 1-5
- **Strategic Fit**: 1-5
- **Feasibility**: 1-5
- **Confidence**: 0.1 to 1.0 (based on Evidence Confidence)
